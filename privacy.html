Personal-use application

Privacy policy

This policy describes the owner's private installation of Secretary Calendar.

Data access and use

The application uses Google's OAuth authorization to view the owner's calendars and to create, update, and delete events. It reads calendar data only to find events it may need to change and to avoid duplicates, and it writes only events that come from the owner's own emails and chats.

Storage and sharing

Events remain in the owner's Google Calendar. Short records of what was scheduled are kept on the owner's computer. OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored privately on that computer, outside this website.

The scheduling is carried out by an AI agent that the owner runs, so the details of the events it handles may be sent to the AI provider used for that agent. That provider applies its own privacy and retention policies.

This installation does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control and retention

The owner controls the calendar and the local installation. Events are retained until the owner removes them. Access can be revoked in the Google Account connections settings. Revoking access stops future access but does not remove events already in the calendar.

This website

This static information website has no login, upload form, analytics, or tracking scripts. It does not receive calendar data, Google account credentials, or OAuth tokens. The hosting provider may process ordinary web-request information under its own privacy policy.

Questions and updates

This is a single-owner application. The owner maintains its configuration and this policy. Any future change to data use should be reflected here before it is applied.
